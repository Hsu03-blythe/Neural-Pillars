Why this exists

Neuralis Archivum is a personal exploration of how the brain maps to the stories we tell ourselves. This space was created to encourage a moment of reflection on the complexity of our biology.

The goal is to bridge the gap between intricate neuroscience and minimalist design—exploring the pillars of the human brain, one region at a time.
